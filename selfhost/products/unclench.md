# Unclench (unclench.ai)

Source: read-only study of the public site, 2026-11-24. The OpenSEO project context is the working copy; this file is the human-readable summary.

**Product.** One flat-priced plan with two tools: **Lead Finder** reads your site, proposes an ICP, finds matching people with email/phone, and drafts first emails you approve and send from your own mailbox; **Support Agent** is a website chat agent that answers from your site and docs, says "I don't know" rather than guess, and hands off to humans. One record follows a person from lead to customer to support.

**Positioning.** "Find your next 100 customers and support them 24/7." Built for founders doing sales and support themselves. Flat price, no per-seat or per-resolution fees, human-in-the-loop outreach, 2-minute setup, Intercom import.

**Pricing.** Growth $99, Scale $199, Business $399 per month; 7-day trial with card up front; no free tier; free demo at `/free-ai-support-agent` and 15 free tools.

**ICP.** Solo founders and lean teams (small SaaS, app makers, agencies), small support teams, and teams leaving Intercom over per-seat/Fin pricing.

**Competitors.** Support: Intercom (Fin), Zendesk, Help Scout, Chatbase. Lead gen (inferred): Apollo, ZoomInfo, Lusha, Clay.

**Site.** 44 marketing URLs + 22 docs pages; server-rendered. Content is compare/alternatives pages (12), free tools (15), product pages; no blog.
